AI photo scan

Point. Snap. Know.

Take a photo or choose one from your library. Calorify finds each item on the plate and estimates its portion, calories, protein, carbs and fat.

  • Tap any item to fix its name or portion, or remove it.
  • Before you add the meal, the ring shows what's left after it.
  • Add it to breakfast, lunch, dinner or a snack, today or on an earlier day.

AI estimates. You have the final say. Free plan: 3-day trial of photo scans. Premium: unlimited.

Voice logging Premium

Or just say it.

Tap the microphone and say what you ate, the way you'd tell a friend. Check the transcript, edit it if you need to, and Calorify turns it into foods with calories and macros you can review before adding.

  • “I ate an apple for breakfast”
  • “Log 2 slices of pizza for lunch”
  • “Add a cup of coffee”

Water tracking

A glass, a tap.

Add 250 or 500 ml with one tap, or fluid ounces if you use US units. Your daily goal follows your body weight, and a water streak counts the days you reach it.

Streaks and achievements

Keep the streak.

Log on consecutive days to grow your streak. 22 achievements mark streaks from 3 days to a full year, days on your calorie, protein and water goals, and logging milestones.

Questions

Frequently asked

How does Calorify count calories?

Take a photo and Calorify's AI estimates the portion, calories, protein, carbs and fat of each item on the plate. You can also say what you ate, search for a food or enter it yourself. You can check and edit every item before it's added to your day.

Is Calorify free?

Calorify is free to download. The journal, food search, manual entry, reports and water tracking are free, and the free plan includes a 3-day trial of photo scans. Unlimited photo scans, voice logging and AI insights come with Calorify Premium, an auto-renewing monthly or yearly subscription. The app shows the price in your currency before you subscribe.

How accurate are the photo estimates?

They're AI estimates, so treat them as a starting point. Tap any item to fix its name or portion, or remove it, before you add the meal. Calorify is not a medical device and doesn't give medical advice.

Does Calorify work with Apple Health?

Yes, if you want it to. With your permission, Calorify reads your height, weight, date of birth and sex from Apple Health to fill in your profile, and your steps and active energy to show your activity in the journal and reports. Connecting Apple Health is optional.

Which devices and languages does Calorify support?

Calorify runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 17.6 or later, in light or dark appearance, and in 15 languages: English, Turkish, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish, Russian, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese, Arabic and Hindi.

What happens to my food photos and voice recordings?

When you scan a photo or record a voice entry, it's sent to our servers and to OpenAI to estimate the foods and their nutrition values. We don't store voice recordings. We never sell your personal information or share your food logs with advertisers. The Privacy Policy has the details.
